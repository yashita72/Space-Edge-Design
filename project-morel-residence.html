Project Summary

The Morel family required a structural layout change to maximize ambient light and storage in their 1,400 sq.ft. space. Our designers stripped down partition walls, introduced a modular kitchen layout, and styled the rooms with bespoke wardrobes and custom timber accents.

Key Specifications:

  • 10-year factory finish warranty protection.
  • Customized moisture-resistant gypsum false ceiling board.
  • Soft-close tandem drawers in the kitchen core.
  • Anti-scratch matte laminates on the floor-to-ceiling closets.
