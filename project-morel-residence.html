The Morel Residence

A sweeping full-home transformation turning a dated colonial apartment into a curated retreat of brass, velvet, and botanical warmth.

Location
South Mumbai
Area
3,200 sq ft
Duration
6 Months
Year
2025

The Brief

The Morels had lived in this 1970s colonial apartment in South Mumbai for two decades. The space was filled with disparate memories but lacked a cohesive narrative. Our brief was to design a layout that honoured the high ceilings and classic bones of the structure, while layering modern design comforts, deep textures, and unhurried vintage character.

The Challenge

Because of the structural layouts of Horniman-era designs, the load-bearing columns could not be adjusted, which limited open-plan options between the living room and dining room. We solved this by designing a beautiful custom divider screen made of woven cane and warm-brushed brass details, defining functional boundaries while allowing diffused sunset light to filter through.
