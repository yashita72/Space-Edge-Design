The Designer

Spaces That Tell a Story

Arjun Kapoor is a Mumbai-based interior designer known for creating spaces that feel deeply personal, editorially curated, and warmly inhabited. With over a decade of practice spanning residential transformations to boutique hospitality projects, his work is recognized for its signature layering of vintage materials, honest craftsmanship, and restrained luxury.

Trained in architecture at CEPT Ahmedabad and later in interior design at Central Saint Martins London, Arjun returned to Mumbai to establish Space Edge Design in 2013. Each project begins not with mood boards but with conversations — long, unhurried conversations about how people actually live.

He believes the best interiors are never noticed as designed. They are simply felt — as warmth, as calm, as the quiet thrill of being exactly where you want to be.
