Who We Are

India's Trusted Home Design Partner

Founded by lead architect Arjun Kapoor in 2013, Space Edge Design started with a simple vision: to bridge the gap between custom high-end interior aesthetics and modular factory precision. Today, we stand as one of India's most trusted home interiors brands, serving thousands of families.

We operate custom modular manufacturing units equipped with European technology. Every wardrobe, kitchen panel, and storage unit is engineered to resist moisture and wear, allowing us to back our projects with an industry-leading 10-year warranty.

Our process eliminates the stress of interior designing. We align budget, layouts, and construction pipelines under a single, transparent dashboard, delivering on-site handovers within 45 days.

Our Belief
