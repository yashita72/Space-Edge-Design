About Space Edge Design

Space Edge Design is an interior design company dedicated to creating functional, elegant, and personalized spaces. We specialize in modular kitchens, bedrooms, living rooms, wardrobes, TV units, false ceilings, and complete home interiors across Delhi NCR.

Who We Are

Dedicated to Beautiful Living

Space Edge Design is a customer-focused interior design company based in Vasundhara, Ghaziabad. We believe every home should reflect the lifestyle and personality of its owner.

Our team works closely with clients to design beautiful, practical, and comfortable interiors using quality materials and thoughtful planning.

Whether it's a single room renovation or a complete home interior, we aim to deliver spaces that combine style, functionality, and attention to detail.

Our Mission

"To create elegant and functional interiors that enhance everyday living while delivering quality craftsmanship and a smooth customer experience."
